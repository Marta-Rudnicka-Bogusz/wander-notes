¿What travel means to me?

I’m a woman fueled by curiosity and guided by kindness. For me, travel is more than a journey—it’s a way of connecting with the world, one story, one flavor, one handshake at a time. Together with my family, I explore new places not just to see them, but to truly experience them. Whether we’re sharing meals with locals, learning a traditional dance, or listening to stories passed down through generations, I approach each encounter with an open heart and open mind. Food is my favorite gateway to culture. I find joy in tasting dishes prepared with love—each one a reflection of the people and their way of life. I believe every flavor has a story, and I’m always eager to listen. But more than anything, I’ve learned that respect is the key to true understanding. To really know a culture, I believe we must first honor its customs, values, and traditions. I want to teach my children that the beauty of our world lies in its diversity—that differences are not to be feared, but embraced and celebrated. My travels aren’t about collecting passport stamps; they’re about building meaningful connections. With every journey, I grow not only as a traveler but as a person—more compassionate, more open, and more committed to creating a world built on understanding and mutual respect.
